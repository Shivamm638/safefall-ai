CONFUSION MATRIX ANALYSIS - SafeFall AI test set
====================================================================
Test frames analysed              : 1,975
Frames that really were a fall    : 429

1. FALL DETECTION (the safety-critical row)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
   Correct fall detections        : 401 of 429 (93.5% recall / sensitivity)
   Missed falls (false negatives) : 28 (6.5% of all falls)
   False alarms (false positives) : 124 (8.02% of all non-fall frames)
   Specificity on non-fall frames : 91.98%

   Reading: recall matters most here. Every missed fall is an elderly
   resident left on the floor unattended, whereas a false alarm only
   costs a caregiver a few seconds to dismiss.

2. WHERE THE FALL ALERTS COME FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------
       93 frames of 'Normal Activity' were alerted as a fall (36.76% of that activity)
       28 frames of 'Sitting' were alerted as a fall (8.62% of that activity)
        2 frames of 'Standing' were alerted as a fall (0.37% of that activity)
        1 frames of 'Walking' were alerted as a fall (0.24% of that activity)

3. WHICH ACTIVITIES THE MODEL CONFUSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
       93 frames (36.76%) of 'Normal Activity' predicted as 'Fall Detected'
       37 frames (8.73%) of 'Walking' predicted as 'Standing'
       36 frames (14.23%) of 'Normal Activity' predicted as 'Sitting'
       34 frames (6.25%) of 'Standing' predicted as 'Walking'
       28 frames (8.62%) of 'Sitting' predicted as 'Fall Detected'

   Reading:
   - Normal Activity vs Fall Detected: Normal Activity covers bending and
     crouching, which are exactly the postures halfway between upright and
     on-the-floor. The model errs toward raising an alert, which is the
     correct bias for a fall monitor.
   - Walking vs Standing: Standing and Walking differ only by stance width
     in a still image - a mid-stride frame and a stood-with-feet-apart
     frame are the same posture. Both are safe states, so this costs
     nothing clinically, and the video pipeline resolves it using measured
     motion.
   - Normal Activity vs Sitting: Crouching and perching on the edge of a
     seat produce almost the same knee and hip angles.
   - Sitting vs Fall Detected: Sitting on a chair and sitting on the floor
     after a fall both show a collapsed body height with bent knees. The
     distinguishing cue is how close the hips are to the ankles, which is
     subtle and camera dependent. Over-alerting here is the safe direction
     of error.
   - 170 of the 322 errors (53%) are swaps between two safe activities,
     which never change the alert the caregiver sees.

4. PER-CLASS RECALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
   Fall Detected      93.47%   (support 429 frames)
   Normal Activity    39.53%   (support 253 frames)
   Sitting            89.85%   (support 325 frames)
   Standing           90.81%   (support 544 frames)
   Walking            86.32%   (support 424 frames)
